## ONE product; differentiated entry points

**REG — Beyond the checklist.** "Your system works. Until an assumption doesn't."

REG is a service for finding **adjacent assumptions required by intended system behavior**. The buyer selects source frameworks as discovery triggers, not as proof of legal applicability or operational deficiency. REG can be embedded later, only by request. TENIR-Gov is independently optional and is not required to deliver the Challenge.

The segmentation key is **buyer × consequential behavior × evidence of commercial urgency × reachable sponsor**, **not country stereotypes** or GDP.

## Legal triage in the sales process (no legal determinations by REG)

For **each** referenced text, retain these fields and make an explicit `not_assessed` default:
- jurisdiction, legal nature (binding / voluntary / draft), exact version and effective date;
- entity role (provider, deployer, controller, processor, financial entity, vendor, etc.);
- geographic nexus and where outputs are used (where relevant);
- activity / system category and exemptions; human consequences;
- who has authority to confirm applicability (internal counsel / compliance).

Important:
- EU AI Act art. 2 includes several cross-border scope cases, **not** automatic inclusion for every Moroccan export supplier.
- GDPR has establishment, targeting and monitoring rules and processor contractual duties. An EU principal's procurement request is **not itself** proof that all articles apply to its foreign vendor.
- AI Act milestones are progressive and changed by 2026 Omnibus. Check consolidated version and effective-date metadata before pitching.
- Morocco Law 09-08 applies in its own scope. Do not market EU law as a substitute.
- Regulator references are marketing context, **not** endorsement or certification.

## Distribution sequence without three-country launch overhead

**Wave 1 (days 1–10): 6 warm / reachable conversations**
- Morocco: 3 nearshore/digital operator intros; 1 local fintech through contacts.
- France: 1 regulated B2B software vendor.
- Spain: 1 engineering/payment platform contact.
Criteria: executive sponsor **and** architect to articulate a consequential behavioral claim; otherwise defer.

**Wave 2 (days 11–20): 6 demos**
- Alternate A: human intervention too late; B: ambiguous commit/retry.
- Ask for one explicit claimed guarantee, discover one adjacent dependency, track whether the finding changes a decision.
- Offer anonymized example report; never call it a customer case.
- Offer Express to budget-sensitive leads without compromising rigor or giving the full Focused Challenge for free.

**Wave 3 (days 21–30): 2–3 written proposals**
- Sell outcome and work product, not a REG/ TENIR installation.
- Deliver fully remote/asynchronously except short, scheduled interactions.
- Record sponsor, buying authority, actual objection, reasonable price expectation, decision consequence and referral potential.

**Qualification score** (0–2 per dimension; max 12, no individual lead data in public repo):
1. Consequential behavior clearly named.
2. Business impact of failure understood.
3. Adjacent assumptions plausibly unexamined.
4. Decision or contract due within 90 days.
5. Budget holder or channel sponsor reachable.
6. Scope can be shared in redacted form and assessed without production access.

Prioritize **≥9/12** for paid proposals. This score is a sales triage, **not** a risk, conformance, regulatory or safety score.

## Go / change / stop gates (D30)
- **GO** if at least 5 independent demonstrations show a *new, decision-relevant* assumption; two customers accept a written scoped offer; first paid assignment achieved or one clearly identified near-term close with named sponsor.
- **NARROW** if findings are generic or redundant: specialize in one family (oversight timing or end-to-end idempotence) rather than adding jurisdictions/frameworks.
- **REVISE GTM** if stakeholders buy legal summaries or broad infra audits instead of behavioral challenges; that is a different product and should not be conflated with REG.
- **NO CLAIMS** of product-market fit, certification, ROI or proven prices until customer evidence exists.
